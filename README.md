# Workout Tracker
 
> **Demo only.** This is a demo of the app, not the final version. The pages, posts, profiles, and numbers are fake, nothing is saved, and there is no cloud connection. Refreshing a page resets everything. The final app will be built later.
 
The app name is not final. "Workout Tracker" is a placeholder.
 
## Description
Workout Tracker is a free, cloud-based workout tracker that lets people log their training without a paywall and without having to rely on a notes app or a paper notebook. The app is meant to work on both a phone and a laptop.
 
Users can build workouts from a catalog of exercises or create their own custom exercises. They can log their weight, sets, and reps, and see what they did last session. There is also a social side where users can share workouts and post progress photos. An AI assistant is planned to suggest alternative exercises when a machine is busy, warn about overusing a muscle, and suggest a better exercise order.
